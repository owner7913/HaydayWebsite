Blackjack rules

This table uses site coins only. Nothing here is tied to real money, real prizes, or cash value.

  • Your goal is to beat the dealer by getting closer to 21 without going over.
  • Number cards keep their value, face cards are worth 10, and aces count as 1 or 11.
  • A natural blackjack is an ace plus any 10-value card and pays 3:2 unless the dealer also has blackjack.
  • The dealer reveals the hidden card after player actions and draws until reaching at least 17.
  • Hit takes one more card. Stand keeps your total.
  • Double adds one extra bet, deals exactly one final card, then locks the hand.
  • Split is available on matching opening ranks and turns them into two separate hands with matching bets.
  • Insurance appears only when the dealer shows an ace. It costs half your original bet and pays 2:1 if the dealer has blackjack.
  • Your Table P/L tracks how much you are up or down since you joined this table.
